Decompose and verify ⏱️ – min read

🔁 Recursive Prompting

Your first prompt rarely gets the output you want, and a Large Language Model (LLM) won't tell you what it misunderstood. Recursive prompting treats the prompt as a draft: you test it, see what's wrong with the output, revise the prompt, and repeat until the result is right.

Why it works

  • Each output shows what's missing. Reading the reply tells you exactly which instruction to add next.
  • Small changes are easy to judge. Changing one thing per round shows you what each instruction actually does.
  • The final prompt is reusable. Once the output is right, you keep a prompt that produces it again.

Step by step

Every round below works on the same task: “Write a short product update about our app's new offline mode.” Each revised prompt is shown with its output, next to the output of the round before.

1 Vague prompt 😕

UserWrite a quick update about our app's new offline mode.
ModelWe launched offline mode. It's great, give it a try!

The reply is generic: no benefit, no steps, nothing a user could act on.

2 Add clear constraints ✍️

UserWrite a 30–50 word update about our app's new offline mode for end users. Include the key benefit and how to turn it on, and avoid jargon.
Before · round 1 · ModelWe launched offline mode. It's great, give it a try!
After · round 2 · ModelOffline mode lets you keep working without internet and syncs when you're back online. To enable it, open Settings and turn on Offline Mode. This reduces interruptions and helps you capture ideas anywhere.

3 Add structure and a tone example 🧩

UserWrite a three-sentence update about our app's new offline mode: what changed, why it matters for productivity, and how to turn it on in Settings. Keep the tone warm and concise, like this example: “We built this to help you focus.”
Before · round 2 · ModelOffline mode lets you keep working without internet and syncs when you're back online. To enable it, open Settings and turn on Offline Mode. This reduces interruptions and helps you capture ideas anywhere.
After · round 3 · ModelWe're introducing offline mode so you can keep writing without an internet connection. It reduces interruptions and keeps your notes synced the moment you're back online. To try it, open Settings → Features and switch on Offline Mode.

4 Add a self-check 🔎

UserSame as round 3, plus: before you finish, check that it has three sentences, includes the benefit and the steps to turn it on, and uses no jargon. If anything fails, fix it once.
Before · round 3 · ModelWe're introducing offline mode so you can keep writing without an internet connection. It reduces interruptions and keeps your notes synced the moment you're back online. To try it, open Settings → Features and switch on Offline Mode.
After · round 4 · ModelWe're introducing offline mode so you can capture ideas without an internet connection. It keeps your notes synced when you're back online, reducing interruptions and helping you stay focused. To try it, open Settings → Features and switch on Offline Mode.

The check passes, so the changes are small: the update is now polished and ready to send.

Choosing a stop rule ✅

  • Stability. Two rounds in a row produce nearly the same output.
  • Cost. The improvements no longer justify another round.
