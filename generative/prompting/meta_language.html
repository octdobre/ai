🔣 Meta Language Creation Pattern

Describing the same kind of request in full sentences every time is slow, and long descriptions leave room for misreading. The meta language creation pattern teaches a Large Language Model (LLM) your own shorthand once, and from then on one line of notation is enough.

Why it works

  • Complex relationships fit on one line. A route, a set of durations or a chain of steps becomes a few characters.
  • Repeated requests get faster. You explain the notation once, then reuse it as often as you like.
  • The meaning is exact. Your rules define what each symbol means, so the model interprets every line the same way.

Template

User · templateWhen I say "CityA,N->CityB,M", I mean my route goes from CityA to CityB, and I will stay N days in CityA and M days in CityB

Usage example

1 Define the notation

Set up the task and explain what the shorthand means:

UserWe are going to create a trip planning application. I will describe my trip and you will list interesting things to do in places that I pass through. I am going to use shorthand notation.

When I say "Florence,3->Rome,2", I mean my route will go from Florence to Rome and I will stay 3 days in Florence and 2 days in Rome.
ModelGot it. Send me a route in that notation, and I'll list things to do in each place for the number of days you stay.

2 Use it

From then on, a single line of shorthand is enough:

UserFlorence,3->Rome,2->Naples,1
ModelFlorence (3 days):
  • Visit the Uffizi Gallery to see Renaissance masterpieces
  • Climb the Duomo for panoramic city views
  • Explore the Boboli Gardens
  • Walk across Ponte Vecchio at sunset
Rome (2 days):
  • Tour the Colosseum and Roman Forum
  • Visit Vatican City and the Sistine Chapel
  • Throw a coin in the Trevi Fountain
  • Enjoy authentic carbonara in Trastevere
Naples (1 day):
  • Sample authentic Neapolitan pizza at its birthplace
  • Take a quick day trip to Pompeii
  • Stroll along the waterfront promenade

The model read three cities and three stay lengths from one line, and sized each list to the number of days.

Before and after

The same line of shorthand, sent without the definition and after it:

UserFlorence,3->Rome,2->Naples,1
Before · no notation · ModelIt looks like you've listed three Italian cities with numbers. Do the numbers mean days, rankings, or something else? Let me know what you'd like me to do with this list.

Without the definition, the model can only guess what the numbers and arrows mean.

After · with notation · ModelFlorence (3 days): the Uffizi Gallery, the Duomo, the Boboli Gardens, Ponte Vecchio at sunset. Rome (2 days): the Colosseum, Vatican City… Naples (1 day): pizza, Pompeii, the waterfront.
